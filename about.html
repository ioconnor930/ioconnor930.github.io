Biography

Ian O’Connor (IEEE S’95-M’98-SM’07) is Distinguished Professor for Heterogeneous and Nanoelectronics Systems Design in the Department of Electronic, Electrical and Control Engineering at Ecole Centrale de Lyon, France.

He is joint head of the Electronics group at the Lyon Institute of Nanotechnology, Director of the CNRS SoC2 research network, and scientific co-director of the France 2030 PHOENIX programme. From 2008 to 2022 he was also Adjunct Professor at Ecole Polytechnique de Montréal, Canada. His research interests include novel computing and interconnect architectures based on emerging technologies, associated with methods for design exploration.

He has authored or co-authored over 300 peer-reviewed book chapters, journal publications, conference papers and patents, has held positions of responsibility in the organization of many international conferences, including General Chair of DATE 2023, and has been work-package leader or scientific coordinator for numerous regional, national and European projects.

He serves as an expert with Collège Numérique France 2030, the International Roadmap for Devices and Systems (IRDS) Systems and Architectures International Focus Team, and IFIP WG 10.5 (Design and Engineering of Electronic Systems). He is Vice-President for Publications in the IEEE Council on Electronic Design Automation (CEDA), Chair of the Main Board of the European Design Automation Association (EDAA), and Area Editor (Circuits and Emerging Technologies) for IEEE Transactions on Computer-Aided Design of Integrated Circuits and Systems.
