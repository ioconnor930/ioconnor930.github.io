Teaching

I teach in the Centrale Lyon engineering programme and in master’s programmes, from the fundamentals of electronic systems to hardware architectures for artificial intelligence. I co-manage the Electronics and Embedded Systems track of the Master 2 Electronics, Electrical Energy, Automation (EEEA), co-accredited with Université Lyon 1, and sit on its management board.
