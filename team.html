Team

I have supervised 40 PhD theses, 12 postdoctoral researchers and 28 MSc interns. Former students now work at IBM Research, CERN, EPFL, the University of Utah, NTU Singapore, CEA-LETI, STMicroelectronics, Cadence, Synopsys, Siemens EDA, Corning and ID Quantique, and have founded or joined start-ups including eVaderis and Luceda.
